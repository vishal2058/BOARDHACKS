Education should create capability, confidence, and direction.

I am an entrepreneur driven by a simple belief: education should create capability, confidence, and direction, not just certificates.

As the Founder of MindSkyra, I am building a learning ecosystem that blends technology, practical skills, and disciplined mentorship to create real, measurable transformation in students' lives. My work sits at the intersection of education, technology, and leadership.

Since 2022, I have been deeply involved in teaching, mentoring, curriculum design, and institution building, working hands-on with students in programming, IT skills, and competitive examination preparation including JEE, NEET, MHT-CET, and other academic pathways.

Strong institutions are built by founders who understand learners deeply, design systems over shortcuts, and measure success through long-term outcomes. I am not interested in trends: I care about impact over trends.

My current focus

  • Scalable education models across online and offline learning.
  • AI tools that improve learning efficiency.
  • Future-ready skills, mindsets, and discipline in students.
  • Practical learning that connects knowledge with real-world capability.
  • Sustainable education and technology ventures.

My vision: to build education platforms that do not just teach subjects, but shape thinkers, builders, and leaders.

Open to strategic collaborations, partnerships, and conversations around EdTech, education leadership, artificial intelligence, and skill development.
